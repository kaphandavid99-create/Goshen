GOSHEN PROVISION PROJECT

A Digital Ordering Platform for a Provision Shop in New Bell, Bamenda, Cameroon

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Goshen Provision Project represents a comprehensive digital transformation solution for a traditional provision shop operating in New Bell, Bamenda, Cameroon. This document summarizes the complete design and implementation of an e-commerce platform that addresses the critical business challenge of reduced foot traffic due to increased competition from business relocations caused by ongoing road construction in the area.

The solution consists of a responsive Progressive Web Application (PWA) that provides customers with the ability to browse products, place orders remotely, track shipments, and enjoy loyalty rewards. The platform serves retail customers, wholesale buyers, and internal staff management.

2. BUSINESS CONTEXT & PROBLEM STATEMENT

Background

Bamenda has experienced significant road construction that altered movement patterns and business distribution. This triggered business relocations, creating high business density in specific neighborhoods. Goshen Provision, located in New Bell, faced unexpected competitive pressures.

Main Challenge

The relocation of businesses caused by road construction has increased the concentration of businesses around Goshen Provision, resulting in increased competition and a reduction in the number of customers visiting the shop.

3. IDENTIFIED PROBLEMS

  • Reduced Customer Traffic: Fewer customers visit compared to pre-construction periods
  • Increased Competition: More businesses operating in the same area sell similar products
  • Reduced Visibility: Harder to differentiate among many competitors in congested area
  • Physical Customer Dependency: Business model relies entirely on walk-in foot traffic
  • Limited Geographic Reach: Customers outside the immediate area remain unaware of offerings

4. PROPOSED SOLUTION

Rather than competing on location or price alone in an increasingly crowded retail environment, the solution implements a digital-first, omnichannel strategy that:

  • Creates a permanent digital storefront independent of physical foot traffic
  • Reaches customers wherever they are rather than requiring shop visits
  • Provides multiple fulfillment options (delivery or pickup)
  • Builds customer loyalty through rewards and engagement
  • Enables expansion into new customer segments and geographic areas

Solution Architecture

A Progressive Web Application (PWA) that:

  • Runs in the browser like a website but installs on phones like a native app
  • Works online and offline with intelligent synchronization
  • Provides fast, responsive performance across devices
  • Supports push notifications for customer engagement

6. CORE FEATURES IMPLEMENTATION

01. Online Product Catalog

Products listed with price, unit, category, and photos. Searchable and filterable by category or current deals. Live stock status displayed.

02. Cart & Checkout

Build cart, choose delivery or pickup, see transparent pricing breakdown. Real-time fee calculation and order summary.

03. Cash & Mobile Money Payment

Orders paid on delivery/pickup in cash, or immediately by Mobile Money. Multiple payment options accommodate customer preferences.

04. Loyalty Points Program

Earn 100 points signup bonus, 1.5 pts per 100 FCFA spent, 10 pts per review. Redeem 1 pt = 1 FCFA discount. Minimum 500 pts to redeem.

05. Referral Program

Earn 100 points when friend signs up, another 100 when they order. Turns customer base into organic marketing channel.

06. Push & In-App Notifications

Order updates, abandoned-cart reminders, loyalty nudges, promotions delivered to customer devices. Keeps Goshen visible even when not shopping.

07. AI Shopping Assistant

24/7 chat assistant answers questions about stock, orders, delivery, points. Instant support without human availability needed.

08. Installable Web & Mobile App

Installs to phone/desktop home screen like native app. Persistent presence keeps Goshen visible on customer devices.

09. Wholesale Ordering Portal

Approved business buyers apply for bulk orders at wholesale pricing. New revenue stream from business customers.

10. Bilingual Interface

Entire platform available in English and French. Serves mixed-language Bamenda environment.

11. Admin Management Dashboard

Shop owner manages products, orders, promotions, analytics from single dashboard. Live KPIs and sales analytics.

12. Customer Reviews & Wishlist

Verified buyers rate products building social proof. Wishlist keeps customers engaged between visits.

7. DATABASE DESIGN

Database Choice: PostgreSQL

Selected for four critical reasons:

  • Data Integrity: ACID compliance and foreign-key constraints ensure orders, payments, and loyalty points never become inconsistent
  • Relational Structure: Customer has many orders, orders have many items, items reference products. Perfect relational model fit
  • Mature Hosting: Affordable managed hosting (Supabase) appropriate for small business project
  • Flexibility: JSON columns for flexible fields (drink flavors, flavor breakdowns) while maintaining relational integrity elsewhere

15 Core Database Tables

  • User Accounts: All customers, staff, and admin accounts with authentication and roles
  • Products: Category and Product tables for catalog organization and management
  • Orders: Order, OrderItem, Payment, Address tables for complete fulfillment tracking
  • Cart Tracking: CartSnapshot for server-side shopping cart mirroring
  • Notifications: Notification and PushSubscription for customer messaging delivery
  • AI Assistant: ChatThread and ChatMessage for conversation history
  • Engagement: Review and WishlistItem for trust building and customer retention
  • Wholesale: WholesaleApplication for business buyer applications

ORM Choice: Prisma

Prisma was chosen because it:

  • Generates type-safe database clients from schema files
  • Tracks migrations as reviewable version files
  • Automatically parameterizes queries eliminating SQL injection
  • Reads like the relationships it represents
